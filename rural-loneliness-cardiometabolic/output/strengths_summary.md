# Strengths-based results

## A. Social associations (membership organizations per 10,000)
- Nonmetro 12.8 vs metro 9.8 (medians 12.4 vs 9.6); Welch p = 6.5e-41
- Gradient across RUCC 1→9: Spearman rho = 0.27, p = 8.2e-39
- By RUCC (mean): 1: 9.1, 2: 9.8, 3: 10.7, 4: 11.8, 5: 12.4, 6: 12.3, 7: 13.4, 8: 12.0, 9: 14.0

## B. Rural loneliness advantage
- Given their hardship, nonmetro counties' loneliness is -0.65 pts vs metro (95% CI -0.94 to -0.36, p = 8.5e-06)
- Adding social associations: -0.58 pts; each 1 SD more associations = -0.13 pts loneliness (p = 0.21)

## C. Hardship–loneliness decoupling
- Per 1 SD hardship, loneliness rises 1.64 pts in metro and 1.42 pts in nonmetro (difference p = 0.028)

## D. Buffering (outcome: cardiometabolic burden)
- Hardship slope where loneliness is low (−1 SD): 0.618; high (+1 SD): 0.595 (interaction p = 0.34)
- Hardship × social associations: b = +0.022, p = 0.017 (negative = buffering)

## E. Social associations and cardiometabolic burden
- Per 1 SD associations: metro -0.058, nonmetro -0.032 (difference p = 0.31)

## F. Positive deviance (high-hardship nonmetro counties)
- 777 nonmetro counties with above-median hardship; 195 positive deviants (healthiest quartile relative to expected burden)
- Loneliness: 35.5% vs 35.5%; social associations: 11.6 vs 11.1 per 10,000
- Adjusted OR per 1 SD loneliness = 1.38 (p = 0.41); per 1 SD associations = 1.36 (p = 0.015)
